      How to play

      Peek at the secret word without letting anyone see. Start the timer and draw it. No letters or numbers allowed! When someone guesses right, tap “They guessed it!” and pass the phone.

      Each part has two stages. First answer the question, then pick up your crayon and color the whole part yourself. Finish a part to win a sticker, and finish the level to win a trophy!
